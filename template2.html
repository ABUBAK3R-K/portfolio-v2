about

I’m a B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I got into development by building small projects and gradually became more interested in what happens behind the interface — how data, models, APIs and software can come together to solve real problems. These days, most of my learning happens through building rather than just following tutorials.

I like projects that sit somewhere between experimentation and engineering. I’ll usually start with a simple idea, break it down, build a working version, and then figure out how to make it better. I’m still learning a lot, but I care about understanding why something works rather than just making it work once.

Right now, I’m exploring AI/ML, LLM applications, intelligent agents and production-oriented software. I’m always interested in meeting people who like building things, working on interesting problems, or simply having a good technical conversation.
